Design system

Generated from design/tokens.json, a copy of Journey's Design 2.1. The colours, the type and the shapes are the same in both apps; this one adds five small tints for telling people and categories apart.

Accent colour

{{ACCENT_INTRO}}

{{SYS_ACCENTS}}

Surfaces, status and tints

Neutral greys with a faint cool tint, so the photos stay the most colourful thing on screen. The tints are new: a dot in the calendar, a bar in a chart, always beside a name.

{{SYS_LIGHT}}{{SYS_DARK}}

Type

One family, Plus Jakarta Sans, in five weights. Titles are set tight in ExtraBold. Numbers in sheets line up in columns.

{{SYS_TYPE}}

Shape and spacing

Corner radius by role, and a 4-point spacing scale.

Components

Rendered with the live tokens. They follow the theme switch on the Screens tab. The audience chip is the new one: it sits on everything and says who can see it.
