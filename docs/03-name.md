# The name

> **Status:** decided. You chose **Homeport** on 12 Nov 2026. · **Updated:** 12 Nov 2026
>
> The app, its links and its logo use Homeport from one setting (`brand.chosen` in `design/tokens.json`). The name has **not** been cleared: see "What was checked" below. The repository keeps the name Family-Cloud.

## What was checked on 12 Nov 2026, and what was not

A web search, not a legal search:

- **App stores:** a search for a family organiser or password app called Homeport found none. That is one search, not a look through both stores.
- **Trademarks:** third-party pages that list US trademark records show several HOMEPORT marks, most of them dead. One is listed as live (registration 7625469; the pages don't say for which goods) and one application was filed in June 2026 for a software platform for local business information. No EU filing turned up. These pages are not the official registers.
- **Not checked:** the official registers (USPTO, EUIPO), the App Store and Google Play by hand, the domain `homeport.app`, social handles.

Before the stores: have the name checked properly in the countries you launch in, and decide the domain. If it has to change, it is one setting.

## What the name has to do

1. **Work for friends as well as family.** A flat share, a couple and a group that travels together should all feel it is for them. "Family Cloud" fails this one.
2. **Sit next to Journey.** The two apps have one owner and one account. Said together, the names should sound like a pair.
3. **Suit the look.** The brand colour is Sun, a warm gold.
4. **Be ownable.** A common descriptive phrase ("family cloud", "family vault") can't be protected and is lost in App Store search among dozens of look-alikes.
5. **Be easy to say.** "Keep it in ___" and "It's in ___" have to sound natural, because the app's buttons say exactly that.

## The obvious one that is taken

**Hearth** is the name I would have chosen: the fire a household gathers round, and the perfect partner for Journey. It is taken, and in exactly our field. Hearth Display, Inc. sells a family organiser (a wall display with a companion app on the App Store and Google Play) and trademark listings show it holds both HEARTH DISPLAY and HEARTH for software. I would not build on it.

## Why Porchlight

- It keeps what was good about Hearth (warmth, home, a light) without the conflict.
- The pair tells a story in one breath: **Journey** is the going out, **Porchlight** is the coming home. That is also how the two apps work together: tell a story there, keep it here.
- The logo draws itself: a house with a light on. It sits well beside Journey's route mark on the same gold square.
- It gives the features their words. A light that is on for some people and off for others is the whole sharing model; a guest pass is "leaving the light on" for the sitter.

## Notes stays Notes

You asked on 12 Nov for a catchier name for the Notes place in the top row. I tried **Lore** on the screens (what a family, a flat or a crew knows and tells). You looked and chose to keep **Notes**, the same day. The screens say Notes again.

The label is still one setting (`places.notes.label` in `design/tokens.json`) should you want to try another.

## What I checked, and what I didn't

- I searched the web for each name alongside "family app", "notes", "photos" and "organizer". That is how the Hearth conflict turned up. For Porchlight the nearest thing I found was a real-estate firm's template app, "PorchLight Design Studio".
- **Not done:** a proper trademark search (USPTO, EUIPO and wherever you plan to launch), App Store and Play Store name availability, and domain names. The web addresses on the screens (`porchlight.app` and the others) are placeholders I have not checked.
- A name should be cleared by someone qualified before money is spent on it. Treat everything here as a shortlist, not a clearance.
