# The name

> **Status:** not decided. "Family Cloud" is the working title. · **Updated:** 11 Nov 2026
>
> My pick is **Porchlight**. The screens can show every candidate: use the Name switch on the Screens page.

## What the name has to do

1. **Work for friends as well as family.** A flat share, a couple and a group that travels together should all feel it is for them. "Family Cloud" fails this one.
2. **Sit next to Journey.** The two apps have one owner and one account. Said together, the names should sound like a pair.
3. **Suit the look.** The brand colour is Sun, a warm gold.
4. **Be ownable.** A common descriptive phrase ("family cloud", "family vault") can't be protected and is lost in App Store search among dozens of look-alikes.
5. **Be easy to say.** "Keep it in ___" and "It's in ___" have to sound natural, because the app's buttons say exactly that.

## The candidates

| Name | The idea | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Porchlight** (my pick) | The light someone leaves on so you can find your way home. Journey is where you go; Porchlight is what you come home to. | Warm, and the colour is already right: a porch light is gold. Says "home" and "someone is expecting you" without saying "family", so friends fit. I found no app with this name in the family, notes or photo space. | Three syllables and ten letters, the longest here. A US spelling of a word some write as two ("porch light"). |
| **Homeport** | The harbour a boat belongs to. Every journey leaves from one and returns to it. | The cleanest pairing with Journey. "Home" is in the word. | Feels nautical and a little cold. Used by a few unrelated products (a US Coast Guard site, a network storage app). |
| **Kith** | From "kith and kin": friends and family. It answers your "friends or family" point in one word. | Four letters, and it means exactly the audience. | Many people don't know the word. A large fashion retailer is called Kith: a different business, but a loud one. "Keep it in Kith" is awkward, because kith is people, not a place. |
| **Fold** | Your fold is your people ("back in the fold"). It also folds papers away. | Short, friendly, a small joke about folders. | A US finance app is called Fold, which is close to our Money and Vault features. |
| **Family Cloud** | Says what it is. | Nothing to explain. | Leaves friends out, can't be protected, and "cloud" sounds like storage, which is the least interesting part of the app. |

## The obvious one that is taken

**Hearth** is the name I would have chosen: the fire a household gathers round, and the perfect partner for Journey. It is taken, and in exactly our field. Hearth Display, Inc. sells a family organiser (a wall display with a companion app on the App Store and Google Play) and trademark listings show it holds both HEARTH DISPLAY and HEARTH for software. I would not build on it.

## Why Porchlight

- It keeps what was good about Hearth (warmth, home, a light) without the conflict.
- The pair tells a story in one breath: **Journey** is the going out, **Porchlight** is the coming home. That is also how the two apps work together: tell a story there, keep it here.
- The logo draws itself: a house with a light on. It sits well beside Journey's route mark on the same gold square.
- It gives the features their words. A light that is on for some people and off for others is the whole sharing model; a guest pass is "leaving the light on" for the sitter.

## The place that was called Notes

You asked for something catchier than Notes for that place in the top row. It is now **Lore**.

- Lore is what a group knows and tells: how the house works, Grandma's recipes, what the children said, the crew's private jokes. That is what ends up in there.
- It fits all four kinds of circle: family lore, our lore, house lore, crew lore.
- It is four letters, which matters in a row of seven.

| Other names | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| Pages | Plain and exact | Not catchy; Apple has an app called Pages |
| Handbook | Right for a flat and a house manual | Sounds like work; wrong for a love note |
| Almanac | Charming, a book a household keeps | Long, and not everyone knows the word |
| Fridge | Where a home's notes really live | Sounds like food and nothing else |

It is one setting in `design/tokens.json` (`places.notes.label`). A single thing you write is still called a note or a page.

## What I checked, and what I didn't

- I searched the web for each name alongside "family app", "notes", "photos" and "organizer". That is how the Hearth conflict turned up. For Porchlight the nearest thing I found was a real-estate firm's template app, "PorchLight Design Studio".
- **Not done:** a proper trademark search (USPTO, EUIPO and wherever you plan to launch), App Store and Play Store name availability, and domain names. The web addresses on the screens (`porchlight.app` and the others) are placeholders I have not checked.
- A name should be cleared by someone qualified before money is spent on it. Treat everything here as a shortlist, not a clearance.
