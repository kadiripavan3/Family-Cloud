Delete your account

The quickest way is in the app: You, then Delete account. Your profile and what was yours are gone at once; the files follow within minutes.

What is deleted

Your profile, the things only you could see, and the things you made in your circles. A circle other people are still in stays, without what was yours. For good: it can't be undone.

Your Journey account

Homeport and Journey share one sign-in. Deleting your Homeport account leaves Journey as it is.

If you can't open the app

You can from the address you signed up with. We answer to that address to make sure the request comes from you, and delete the account once you reply.
